02 /THE PERSON BEHIND THE CODE

More than what I build.

✳

Away from the editor, you'll probably find me singing with the University of Pisa choir, playing the violin or travelling around the world.

I like the questions that don't come with obvious answers.

Somewhere along the way, I've learned that some of the most interesting things happen when you step outside what you already know.

My studies at the University of Pisa have taken me from web applications to distributed systems and data analysis. Each project gives me another way to understand software: by making choices, testing them and working out what I could improve.

I’ve learned from teammates at university, colleagues during my Apuliasoft internship and people I met on Erasmus in Hildesheim. I’m keen to keep exploring, listen to different perspectives and put what I learn into practice.
